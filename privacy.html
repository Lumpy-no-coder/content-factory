Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Content Factory is a private tool used only by the owner of the channels it uploads to. It uses Google sign-in (OAuth) and the YouTube Data API with two permissions: upload videos (youtube.upload) and read the channel's own details (youtube.readonly), so it can upload videos and confirm they arrived.

What it stores

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sent to anyone else. The tool does not collect, store, sell or share any data about viewers or any other person.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account, Third-party apps and services.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube's own Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy also apply.

Contact

Questions: use the contact details on the About page of any of the channels above.
